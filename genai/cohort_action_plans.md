## Geriatric Circulatory with Polypharmacy

- **Target Population:** Age 70+, Primary Diagnosis: Circulatory (Heart Failure / CAD)
- **Encounter Volume:** 908 patients
- **30-Day Readmission Rate:** **17.0%** (National Benchmark: 8.0%)
- **Average Length of Stay:** 4.2 days | **Avg Medications:** 15.4
- **Medication Titration Rate:** 44.8%

### Prescribed 3-Line Action Plan

> **1. Risk Etiology:** Advanced age paired with high cardiovascular polypharmacy (15.4 medications) and active dosage titration (44.8% change rate) elevates 30-day readmission to 17.0%.
>
> **2. Pre-Discharge Protocol:** Mandate bedside clinical pharmacist medication reconciliation and bedside heart failure/fluid management education prior to discharge order sign-off.
>
> **3. Post-Acute Cadence:** Schedule transitional telephonic nurse check-in at 48 hours post-discharge and guarantee an in-person cardiology/endocrinology clinic consultation within 7 days.

## Uncontrolled Hyperglycemia with Insulin Titration

- **Target Population:** Severe Hyperglycemia (A1C >7-8%), Active Insulin Dosage Titration
- **Encounter Volume:** 185 patients
- **30-Day Readmission Rate:** **21.1%** (National Benchmark: 8.0%)
- **Average Length of Stay:** 4.7 days | **Avg Medications:** 14.6
- **Medication Titration Rate:** 41.1%

### Prescribed 3-Line Action Plan

> **1. Risk Etiology:** Glycemic volatility requiring rapid insulin titration (41.1% adjustments) leads to post-discharge medication adherence failure and acute hypoglycemia/hyperglycemia relapse (21.1% readmission rate).
>
> **2. Pre-Discharge Protocol:** Deploy Certified Diabetes Care and Education Specialist (CDCES) for 1-on-1 insulin injection self-management training and provide a 30-day home glucometer kit.
>
> **3. Post-Acute Cadence:** Initiate clinical pharmacist telehealth check-ins on Days 3, 7, and 14 for insulin titration, with an outpatient primary care visit within 10 days.

## Frequent Inpatient Recidivism Cohort

- **Target Population:** High Prior Hospitalization Velocity (>= 2 Inpatient Visits in Prior 12 Months)
- **Encounter Volume:** 922 patients
- **30-Day Readmission Rate:** **33.8%** (National Benchmark: 8.0%)
- **Average Length of Stay:** 4.4 days | **Avg Medications:** 15.9
- **Medication Titration Rate:** 44.0%

### Prescribed 3-Line Action Plan

> **1. Risk Etiology:** Frequent hospitalization history (averaging 2.8 prior inpatient stays) reflects severe disease progression and care coordination fragmentation, resulting in an extreme 33.8% readmission rate.
>
> **2. Pre-Discharge Protocol:** Convene multidisciplinary case management huddle to address social determinants of health (SDOH), transportation barriers, and outpatient prescription affordability.
>
> **3. Post-Acute Cadence:** Assign dedicated community health worker for home visit within 72 hours and enroll in hospital-to-home transitional care management (TCM) program.

## Post-Acute Skilled Nursing Facility (SNF) Transition

- **Target Population:** Discharged to Skilled Nursing Facility (SNF) / Sub-Acute Rehabilitation
- **Encounter Volume:** 1,022 patients
- **30-Day Readmission Rate:** **17.5%** (National Benchmark: 8.0%)
- **Average Length of Stay:** 4.3 days | **Avg Medications:** 15.4
- **Medication Titration Rate:** 45.8%

### Prescribed 3-Line Action Plan

> **1. Risk Etiology:** Transition to skilled nursing involves high handover risk, medication transcription errors, and decompensation during physical rehabilitation, driving a 17.5% readmission rate.
>
> **2. Pre-Discharge Protocol:** Implement warm clinical handover with receiving SNF medical director, providing a synchronized 7-day electronic medication reconciliation bundle.
>
> **3. Post-Acute Cadence:** Conduct joint hospitalist-SNF telehealth rounding at 5 days post-transfer to verify clinical stability and prevent acute ER re-transfer.
